About the Project

Plant Disease Detection is an AI-based system designed to identify diseases from plant leaf images.

The system uses a MobileNetV2 deep learning model trained on plant disease images and provides the predicted disease along with confidence, treatment and prevention information.

How It Works

📷
1. Upload

Upload a plant leaf image.

⚙️
2. Preprocess

The image is resized and preprocessed.

🤖
3. Predict

MobileNetV2 predicts the disease.

📊
4. Result

Disease and confidence are displayed.
